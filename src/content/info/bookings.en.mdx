# Appointments

You can display a button or card on your profile that lets visitors schedule time with you using your own calendar service.

## How it works

1. Create a booking link in your calendar service.
2. Add that link as a card or button in the Studio.
3. Visitors schedule directly through that service; the confirmation comes from that service.

## Good to know

ROUT doesn't store any appointments or details of who books. You manage availability, reminders, and cancellations in your calendar service.
