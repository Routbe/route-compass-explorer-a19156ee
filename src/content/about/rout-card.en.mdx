# The ROUT Card

Your profile in a form you can carry: a metal card, a Google Wallet or Apple Wallet pass, and a rotatable 3D card. Issuance depends on the configured provider and account eligibility.

## One design, three formats

The physical card carries public branding, a card number and a printed QR. The Wallet pass follows the public profile design. The 3D viewer shares public front artwork; private authorization tokens must never appear in exported PNGs.

## Three separate scan layers

1. **Printed QR** opens the public card status at `/v/…`. It does not sign anyone in.
2. **NFC handshake** uses cryptographic verification with a compatible reader. It is distinct from the printed QR.
3. **Rotating digital token** is available only to the owner, checked when opened and single-use.

A public image can reveal information the owner chose to publish. Never place private access codes in an image module or press download.

## Card numbers and privacy

Card numbers use a keyed, one-way derivation rather than the internal account ID. Root and alias spaces are separated. A stable pass serial prevents a handle change from abandoning a saved pass.

## Lost card

Freeze the card in your account. Frozen cards must be rejected by verification and receive no new live code. Freezing is not a substitute for reporting a compromised account or rotating affected credentials.

## Google Wallet artwork

The [public PNG collection](/press/wallet) includes logos, hero artwork and image modules. Personalized hero rendering uses public card information only. Adding a pass requires an authorized issuer and service account; artwork alone does not activate issuance.
