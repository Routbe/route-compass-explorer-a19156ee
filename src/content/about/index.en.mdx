# ROUT

An identity that remains yours. Open protocols, understandable boundaries and software that serves people.

## Your name is not platform property

ROUT connects profiles, QR infrastructure and standard OpenID Connect. Autonomy means knowing which data is used and why.

## Principles with practical boundaries

Read the [manifesto](/about/manifesto), [license](/about/open-source) and [sovereignty principles](/about/sovereignty). They describe implementation boundaries, not guarantees of perfect security.

## One ecosystem, not a closed garden

The [ecosystem](/about/ecosystem) separates database, storage, hosting and optional providers. [Brand and design](/about/brand) describes a recognizable and accessible ROUT experience.

## Questions and responsible AI

[Anonymous questions](/about/anonymous-questions) let owners choose their audience and published answers. [Responsible AI](/about/responsible-ai) explains why optional rewriting does not guarantee anonymity.

## What ROUT does

Explore [the platform](/about/platform) and [the ROUT Card](/about/rout-card): profiles, consent, QR codes, links and a public card that does not act as an account password.
